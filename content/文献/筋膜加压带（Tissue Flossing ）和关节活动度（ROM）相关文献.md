**Yao L, Gan J, Romagnoli M, et al. (2026)**

> **Tissue Flossing: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Its Effects on Range of Motion, Pain, Muscle Strength, and Performance**

- **期刊**：_Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research_
    
- **证据等级**：★★★★★（Meta-analysis）
    
- **DOI**：10.1519/JSC.0000000000005340 ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41416873/?fc=20220522063140&ff=20251225084026&v=2.18.0.post22+67771e2&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Tissue Flossing: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Its Effects on Range of Motion, Pain, Muscle Strength, and Performance - PubMed"))
    

### 纳入研究

- 20项随机或交叉试验
    
- 共480名健康受试者
    

### 关于ROM的主要结果

**ROM显著提高，但幅度较小。**

Meta分析结果：

- **Hedges' g = 0.19**
    
- P = 0.01
    
- 属于**小效应（small effect）**
    

作者总结：

> Tissue flossing may acutely enhance joint ROM but does not significantly improve muscle strength or athletic performance. ([Syddansk Universitet](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/tissue-flossing-a-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis-of-its-effe/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Tissue Flossing: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Its Effects on Range of Motion, Pain, Muscle Strength, and Performance - University of Southern Denmark"))

### 额外发现

效果受到：

- 缠绕压力
    
- 缠绕部位
    
- 训练水平
    
- 性别
    

等因素影响。

作者统计：

**15项研究专门观察ROM**

# 目前证据总体结论

|问题|目前证据|
|---|---|
|是否增加ROM|✅ 有，中等证据支持|
|是否长期改善ROM|❌ 证据不足|
|是否改善肌力|❌ 基本无|
|是否提高运动表现|⚠ 结果不一致|
|是否真正"松解筋膜"|❌ 没有直接证据支持|
|最可能机制|神经调节、疼痛敏感性下降、组织滑动改善，而非筋膜结构改变 ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2021.666129/full?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Frontiers \| Effects of Tissue Flossing on the Healthy and Impaired Musculoskeletal System: A Scoping Review"))|

### 对实践的启示

综合现有证据，筋膜加压带更适合作为**训练前的短期辅助手段**，用于暂时提升ROM或改善动作舒适度。若目标是长期提高关节活动度或改善功能，仍应以力量训练、主动活动度训练和针对性的运动控制训练为核心，筋膜加压带可作为这些训练的补充，而非替代。([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41416873/?fc=20220522063140&ff=20251225084026&v=2.18.0.post22+67771e2&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Tissue Flossing: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Its Effects on Range of Motion, Pain, Muscle Strength, and Performance - PubMed"))
